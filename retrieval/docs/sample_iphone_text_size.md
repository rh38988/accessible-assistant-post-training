Change the text size on iPhone

Go to Settings, then Display & Brightness, then Text Size. Drag the slider to make text larger or smaller.

For even larger text, go to Settings, then Accessibility, then Display & Text Size, then Larger Text. Turn on Larger Accessibility Sizes and drag the slider.
