# Start or join a Group FaceTime call

A Group FaceTime call can include up to 32 people.

Start from a group text in Messages:
1. Open **Messages** and open (or start) a group conversation.
2. Tap the **FaceTime** button in the upper-right corner.
3. Tap **FaceTime Video** (or **FaceTime Audio** for voice only).

Start from the FaceTime app: tap **New Call**, add each person's name or number, then tap **FaceTime** for video.

Join a group call someone else started: tap the notification at the top of the screen, or tap **Join** in the group conversation in Messages.

Notes: Older iPhone and iPad models may only be able to join as audio. Group FaceTime is not available in every country or region.
