# Make and answer a FaceTime video call on iPhone or iPad

Prerequisite: FaceTime must be turned on. Open **Settings**, scroll to **Apps**, tap **FaceTime**, and turn FaceTime on.

Make a call:
1. Open the **FaceTime** app.
2. Tap **New Call**.
3. Type the person's name, phone number, or email address. If they are in your contacts, start typing the name and tap it when it appears.
4. Tap **FaceTime** for a video call, or **Audio** for a voice-only call.

Answer a call: tap **Accept**. If a second FaceTime call arrives while you are on one, choose **End & Accept**, **Hold & Accept**, or **Decline**.

Notes: FaceTime works over Wi-Fi or cellular data, and data charges may apply. FaceTime is not available in every country or region.
