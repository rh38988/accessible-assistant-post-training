Topic: sending money with payment apps (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal)

# Use payment apps safely

Before you send money:
- Never pay to collect a prize or sweepstakes winnings.
- Never give your app login to anyone who contacts you.
- Turn on multi-factor authentication or a PIN for the app.
- Check the person's details before you pay so the money goes to the right person.
- If someone you know asks for money out of the blue, contact them another way to make sure it is really them. Their account may have been hacked.

If you sent money to a scammer, contact the app right away:
- Cash App: in the app, go to your profile and choose **Support**, or visit cash.app/help.
- Venmo: in the app, go to your profile and choose **Get Help**.
- PayPal: use the Resolution Center at paypal.com/disputes.

Then report it to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
