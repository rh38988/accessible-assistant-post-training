# What to do if you were scammed

If you paid a scammer, contact the company that moved the money right away and ask for it back:
- Credit card: call the card issuer and ask for a refund.
- Debit card: call your bank or credit union and ask for a refund.
- Bank transfer such as Zelle: call your bank or credit union and ask them to reverse the payment.
- Wire transfer: call the wire transfer company, say a scammer tricked you, and ask for a refund.
- Gift card: call the gift card company using the number on the back of the card. Keep the card and the receipt.
- Payment app (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal): report it in the app and ask them to reverse the payment.
- Cryptocurrency: contact the exchange or crypto ATM operator and ask them to reverse it.
- Cash sent by U.S. mail: call the U.S. Postal Inspection Service at 1-877-876-2455; for a fee they may be able to stop the package.

If you let a scammer into your computer or phone (remote access):
1. Update your security software.
2. Run a scan and delete anything it finds.
3. Change your passwords and turn on two-factor authentication.

If you gave a scammer your username and password: if you can still sign in, make a new strong password and turn on two-factor authentication. If someone used your Social Security number, report it and get a recovery plan at IdentityTheft.gov.

Report the scam to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
