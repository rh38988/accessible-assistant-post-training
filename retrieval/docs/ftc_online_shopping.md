Shopping online safely

Search the store name with words like scam or complaint. Read reviews from several sources. Check the full price and the return policy. Pay by credit card. Be cautious if a seller insists on gift cards, wire transfers, payment apps, or cryptocurrency. Keep records and report fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
