Family emergency (grandparent) scams

A caller pretends to be a relative in trouble and asks for money urgently, often by gift card, wire transfer, or cash, and may ask you to keep it secret. Do not act right away. Hang up and call the relative at a number you already know, check with other family members, and report the call at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
