## Device Basics

### CT-01 Make text bigger
- **Risk level:** low
- **Platforms:** Android (Pixel); LINE; Samsung Galaxy; Windows 11; iPhone (en;zh-TW; 18 examples)
- **Task IDs:** TR-001, TR-021, TR-022, TR-023, TR-098
- **Expected result:** Text appears larger right away; the sample text changes as the slider moves.
- **Common failure:** Very large text can make buttons hard to tap; some Android apps ignore the font size setting.
- **Sources:**
  - [Samsung Support: How to adjust the font size and style on your Galaxy phone](https://www.samsung.com/latin_en/support/mobile-devices/how-to-adjust-the-font-size-and-style-on-your-galaxy-phone/)

  - [Android Accessibility Help: Change text & display settings](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/11183305?hl=en)
  - [LINE 說明中心：聊天室背景及顯示設定的變更方法](https://help.line.me/line/smartphone/pc?lang=zh-Hant&contentId=20005811)
  - [Microsoft Support: Make text and apps bigger](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/topic/c3095a80-6edd-4779-9282-623c4d721d64)

### CT-13 Someone claiming to be family or a friend asks for money
- **Risk level:** high
- **Platforms:** LINE; any (en;zh-TW; 9 examples)
- **Task IDs:** TR-014, TR-039, TR-112
- **Expected result:** The user pauses, calls the person at a number they already have, and doesn't send money.
- **Common failure:** The voice sounds real (voice cloning), or money or codes were already sent.
- **Sources:**
  - [FTC: Talking Turkey (family emergency scams)](https://www.ftc.gov/node/54353)

- **Risk level:** high
