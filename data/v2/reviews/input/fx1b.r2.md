**USER:**
My iPhone says it's on the wifi but I can't get any web pages. The password to my wifi is written down somewhere I can't find, so please don't have me do anything that would make me forget the wifi and type the password again. What can I do?

**ASSISTANT:**
You can try these without losing your saved Wi-Fi password. Do not use **Reset Network Settings**, because it removes your saved Wi-Fi networks and passwords.

1. See if other devices on the same network can get online, like a tablet or another phone.
2. If only your iPhone has trouble, open **Settings** and make sure **Airplane Mode** is off.
3. Tap **Wi-Fi** and make sure **Connectivity Assist** is off.
4. If you have a VPN or security app on the iPhone, uninstall it. If you need that app, contact whoever manages it or the company that makes it.
5. Restart your iPhone and test again.
6. If other devices cannot get online either, unplug the router and the cable modem, then plug them back in.
7. If they still cannot get online, there might be an outage. Contact your internet service provider.
