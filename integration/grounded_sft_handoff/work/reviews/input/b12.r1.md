  > On iPhone: If the device is off or offline, it only appears online and in Lost Mode if the device has been turned on and is connected to Wi-Fi or cellular.   > On Android: 1. Open the Find Hub app on another Android phone or tablet, or use Find Hub in a browser. 2. Sign in, then select the lost device. 3. Select 'Play sound'. It rings the device at full volume for 5 minutes, even on silent or vibrate.

TARGET (read only after step 2)

> Here is how to mark your missing iPhone as lost, using Find My on your iPad:
> 1. In the **Find My** app, go to the **Devices** tab.
> 2. Scroll down and select your missing iPhone.
> 3. Tap **More**.

> 5. If you want to show your contact information on the device, follow the onscreen steps.
> 6. Tap **Activate**.
