> Here is how to turn on ChromeVox, the built-in screen reader, on a Chromebook:
> 
> 1. Select the time at the bottom right, or press **Alt** + **Shift** + **s**.
> 2. Select **Settings**, then **Accessibility**.
> 3. Under 'Text-to-Speech,' turn on **ChromeVox**.
