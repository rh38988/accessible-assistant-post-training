TARGET (read only after step 2)

> Here is how to make your whole Chromebook screen bigger:
> 1. Select the time at the bottom right, or press **Alt + Shift + S**.
> 2. Select **Settings**, then **Accessibility**.
> 3. Under **Display**, turn on **Full screen magnifier**.
> 4. Choose how the magnified view moves with your mouse: **Move screen continuously as mouse moves**, **Move screen keeping mouse at center of screen**, or **Move screen when mouse touches edge of screen**.
> 5. To increase magnification, press **Ctrl + Alt + Brightness up**. To decrease it, press **Ctrl + Alt + Brightness down**.
