- route: answer
- decision basis (writer's): The user states Gmail on a computer and a recent deletion, so the Gmail Trash steps apply. The Outlook.com ref is for another service and is not used in the reply.

Conversation (the model sees only the user turns, joined):
> **user:** I accidentally deleted an email with my class schedule in it yesterday. I'm using Gmail on my computer. How do I get it back?

Passages the model sees (in this order):
- [recover_deleted_email-gmail-computer-trash] platform: Computer | app: Gmail
  > On Gmail on a computer: Go to Gmail on your computer. On the left, click 'More', then 'Trash'. Check the box to the left of each message you want to recover. At the top, click 'Move to'. In the 'Move to' menu, choose the folder where you want the messages to go, such as your inbox. When you delete a message in Gmail, it's moved to Trash. Up to 30 days after deletion, you can find the message in Trash. After 30 days, the message is permanently deleted and can't be recovered. Deleted drafts can't be recovered from Trash.
- [recover_deleted_email-outlook-web-deleted-items] platform: Outlook.com | app: Outlook.com
  > On Outlook.com: Open the Deleted Items folder and check whether the message is there. If it is, select the message and select 'Restore'. If the message isn't in Deleted Items, open that folder. Above the message list, select 'Recover items deleted from this folder'. If you find the message, select it and then select 'Restore'. Messages removed from the Deleted Items folder are recoverable for 30 days.

Source references to check:
- recover_deleted_email-gmail-computer-trash: Google, "Delete messages in Gmail - Computer - Gmail Help" <https://support.google.com/mail/answer/7401?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop> | section: Remove messages from your trash; Learn what happens when you delete a message | applicability: Gmail on a computer; time limit stated on the page | excerpt: "You can recover the message from your trash and move it back to your inbox."
- recover_deleted_email-outlook-web-deleted-items: Microsoft, "Email is missing from your Outlook.com inbox | Microsoft Support" <https://support.microsoft.com/office/c70b4a9a-caa5-4e8c-a88a-3372215af25e> | section: Check the Deleted Items folder | applicability: Outlook.com web steps; time limit stated on the page | excerpt: "Messages removed from the Deleted Items folder are recoverable for 30 days."

TARGET (read only after step 2)

> Yes, you can bring it back from Trash if it's recent.
> 1. On the left, click **More**, then **Trash**.
> 2. Check the box to the left of the message you want to recover.
> 3. At the top, click **Move to**.
> 4. In the **Move to** menu, choose the folder where you want the message to go, such as your inbox.
> You can find the message in Trash up to 30 days after deletion. After 30 days, it is permanently deleted and can't be recovered.
