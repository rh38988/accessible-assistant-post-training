> Here is how to add a USB printer on Windows 11.
> 1. Make sure the printer is turned on.
> 2. Open **Start**, then **Settings**, then **Bluetooth & devices**, then **Printers & scanners**.
> 3. If it isn't in the list, go to **Add a printer & scanner** and select **Add device**.
> 4. Wait for the list to load, then select the **Add Device** button next to your printer.
> 5. After installation, check **Windows Update** for newer drivers.

TARGET (read only after step 2)

> Here is how to turn on Wi-Fi Calling on your iPhone.
> 1. Open the **Settings** app.
> 2. Tap **Cellular**.
> 3. If your iPhone has multiple SIMs (SIM cards), scroll to the line you want to use.
> 4. Tap **Wi-Fi Calling** and turn on **Wi-Fi Calling on This iPhone**.
> 5. Tap **Enable**. You might need to enter or confirm your address for emergency services.
> If Wi-Fi Calling is available, you'll see 'Wi-Fi' in the status bar while viewing Control Center.
