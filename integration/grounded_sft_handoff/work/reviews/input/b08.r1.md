  > On iPhone: Open Settings, tap Apps, then tap Phone. Choose how to handle calls from numbers not saved in your contacts. Scroll to Screen Unknown Callers and select one of three options. > You can have calls from unsaved numbers sent straight to voicemail on your iPhone.
> 1. Open **Settings**.
> 2. Tap **Apps**.
> 3. Tap **Phone**.
> 4. Scroll to **Screen Unknown Callers**.
> 5. Select **Silence**.
> Calls from unknown numbers are silenced and sent to voicemail. Call screening may incur standard calling and data rates. While you are roaming, calls are not screened, regardless of this setting. If you call emergency services, call screening turns off for 24 hours.
