TARGET (read only after step 2)

> Yes, you can limit the reply to your contacts.
> Here is how to set this up in Gmail on your computer:
> 1. Open **Gmail** on your computer.
> 2. At the top right, click the **Settings** icon, then click **See all settings**.
> 3. On the **General** tab, scroll to the **Vacation responder** section.
> 4. Select **Vacation responder on**.
> 5. Enter December 1 as the start date and December 8 as the end date, then enter a subject and a message.
> 6. To send replies only to your contacts, check the box below the message.
> 7. At the bottom of the page, click **Save Changes**.
> Your automatic reply starts at 12:00 AM on the start date and ends at 11:59 PM on the end date, unless you end it earlier.
