Passages the model sees (in this order):
- [popups-chrome-block-site] platform: Computer | app: Chrome
  > On Chrome on a computer: Open Chrome and click More, then Settings. Click Privacy and security, then Site Settings, then Pop-ups and redirects. Under 'Allowed to send pop-ups and use redirects,' find the site. Click More next to the site, then Block. Source references to check:
- popups-chrome-block-site: Google, "Block or allow pop-ups in Chrome" <https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95472> | section: Manage pop-ups & redirects for a specific site: Block pop-ups and redirects from a site | applicability: Chrome on a computer; steps are listed for the computer | excerpt: "By default, Google Chrome blocks pop-ups from automatically showing up on your screen."

TARGET (read only after step 2)

> Here's how to block pop-ups from one website in Chrome on your computer:
> 1. Open Chrome and click **More**, then **Settings**.
> 2. Click **Privacy and security**, then **Site Settings**, then **Pop-ups and redirects**.
> 3. Under 'Allowed to send pop-ups and use redirects,' find the site.
> 4. Click **More** next to the site, then **Block**.
> 5. If the site isn't listed, click **Add** next to 'Not allowed to send pop-ups or use redirects,' enter the site's web address, and then click **Add**.
